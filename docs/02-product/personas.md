# Personas

**Owner:** UX Researcher

## P1: "Weekend Starter" Sam (primary)

- 34, office job, bought an acoustic guitar 3 weeks ago, has watched some videos.
- **Goal:** play a few recognisable tunes and the four-chord loop around a campfire.
- **Frustrations:** can't tell whether a chord is "right", gets bored with drills, has sore fingertips.
- **Needs:** Wait Mode, "what's wrong" hints, quick wins, short sessions on a phone propped on a music stand.

## P2: "Returning Player" Priya

- 47, played as a teenager, picked up an electric guitar again.
- **Goal:** rebuild clean chord changes and timing.
- **Needs:** play-along with tempo control and loops, timing feedback, being able to skip the basics.

## P3: "Curious Teen" Leo

- 15, borrowed a classical guitar, motivated by game-like progress.
- **Needs:** stars, XP, streaks, a fast feedback loop, works on a cheap Android phone, free.

## P4: "Lefty" Jordan (accessibility and edge cases)

- Left-handed, has red-green colour blindness.
- **Needs:** mirrored diagrams and fretboard, feedback that doesn't rely on red and green.
