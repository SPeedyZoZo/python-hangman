# Pedagogy research

**Owner:** Guitar Educator · **Feeds:** the [curriculum](../05-content/curriculum.md)

## Consensus beginner progression

Beginner curricula broadly agree on this order. It's a starting point, not a standard.

1. **Orientation:** holding the guitar, string names and numbers (1 = high e), reading tab, and picking single open strings.
2. **Single-note melodies** in first position on the top three strings. Quick wins with familiar tunes.
3. **First chords: Em and Am.** They use the fewest fingers and build strength without overloading coordination.
4. **D, G, C.** They open up a huge number of songs. **G–D–Em–C** underpins countless pop songs, and the key of G suits beginners.
5. **Strumming:** start with down-strums on every beat, then the pattern **down, down-up, up-down-up**.
6. **Chord changes as their own skill:** "one-minute changes" between pairs (for example G↔D), repeated.
7. **E and A,** then a **mini F** before the full barre.

## Principles adopted

- **Short sessions:** 2–5 minute lessons, each with one objective.
- **Wait first, then time:** new material is introduced in Wait Mode and timed play-along comes later.
- **Slow is smooth:** default tempo 70% for new content, with a nudge to raise it after a strong result.
- **Name the fix:** feedback says _what_ to change ("one fret too high", "the G string is muted").
- **Celebrate quickly:** stars, XP and streaks, plus a first win within 2 minutes.
- **Comfort:** sore fingertips are normal, so the coach suggests breaks and gives a "fingers hurt" answer.
