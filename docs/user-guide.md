# Strumly user guide

**Owner:** Technical Writer

## 1. Getting started

1. Open Strumly in a recent browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). To install it, use your browser's "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
2. The first launch walks you through a short setup: your experience, guitar type, which hand strums, a **microphone check** and an optional **tune-up**.
3. Put your phone or laptop **30–60 cm from the guitar** in a quiet room. Electric guitars should play through an amp or speaker.

## 1b. Using Strumly on a phone (recommended)

- **Install it:** Android Chrome → menu → _Install app_. iPhone Safari → Share → _Add to Home Screen_. It then opens full-screen like a normal app and works offline.
- **Prop the phone** on a music stand or against something, 30–60 cm from the guitar, screen facing you. Portrait and landscape both work. Landscape gives the highway more room.
- The **screen stays on** while you practise. If the phone locks or you switch apps, the lesson **pauses** and waits for you.
- The controls sit at the bottom, within thumb reach. Extra options are under **More**.
- Tap **⛶ Full screen** in the player to hide the browser bars (Android and desktop; installed apps are already full-screen).
- No guitar handy? Use the **on-screen guitar**. Only the frets you need are shown, and each tap gives a short buzz. Turn it off in Settings → _Vibrate on taps_.

## 2. Tune up

**Tuner** tab → _Start listening_ → pluck one string at a time. Turn the peg until the needle sits
in the green zone and you see **✓ In tune**. Tap a string button to hear its reference note.

## 3. Lessons

The **Learn** tab shows your path: 5 units, 21 lessons. Earn at least one ★ to unlock the next
lesson (returning players can unlock everything in Settings). Each lesson starts with tip cards,
then the **tab highway**:

- Each lane is a string. The **top lane is string 1** (thinnest, high e), just like tab.
- The number on a gem is the **fret** (0 = open). Tall bars are **chords**, with ↓/↑ strum arrows.
- Play when the note reaches the **orange line**. "Heard:" shows what Strumly heard.

### Wait Mode vs Play-along

|          | Wait Mode                            | Play-along                                |
| -------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Music    | Stops on each note until you play it | Keeps moving at the tempo                 |
| Scoring  | Penalises wrong attempts             | Perfect, good, early, late or miss timing |
| Best for | Learning shapes and positions        | Building rhythm and confidence            |

### Practice tools

- **Tempo** −/+ (50–100%). **Loop** a range of bars. **Listen** to a reference performance.
- **Metronome** with a count-in (Play-along). **On-screen guitar**: tap frets to play without an instrument.

## 4. Results and coach

After each run you get stars, accuracy, a breakdown, XP and your streak, plus up to three **coach
tips** based on what happened ("A fret too high", "Start moving to the next chord on the last
beat", "In D, the F♯ isn't ringing…"). For general questions, **Ask the coach** on the Learn tab
answers common beginner questions offline.

## 5. Chords

The **Chords** tab shows diagrams (● finger with its number, ○ open string, × don't play). Open a
chord to **Listen** and **Check my chord**: strum once and Strumly tells you whether it sounds right
or which note is missing.

## 6. Settings

Strumming hand (mirrors diagrams), unlock all lessons, **detection sensitivity** (try _Easy_ if
correct notes go unheard), **timing calibration** (if Play-along always says "late"), on-screen
guitar, metronome, theme, note labels (fret numbers or note names), reset progress, and privacy.

## 7. Privacy

Strumly only uses the microphone while a practice, tuner or chord-check screen is open. Audio is
analysed on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Progress stays in your browser.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem                         | Try                                                                      |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "Microphone access was blocked" | Allow the mic in your browser's site settings, then reload               |
| Correct notes aren't heard      | Tune up. Move closer. Set sensitivity to _Easy_. Reduce background noise |
| Always "late" in Play-along     | Increase the timing calibration in Settings (Bluetooth audio adds delay) |
| No sound from Listen            | Check your device volume and silent switch                               |
